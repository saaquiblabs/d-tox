# D-Tox

D-Tox for YouTube keeps YouTube to what you came for. It hides the parts of YouTube that pull you elsewhere, and it stays measurably light.

## Language

### Product and people

**D-Tox**:
The product, written "D-Tox for YouTube" in full. `d-tox` appears only in URLs, the repo and package names.
_Avoid_: Dtox, DeTox, D-tox

**Learner**:
The primary user: someone who needs YouTube to learn or work and keeps getting pulled off course. Their needs win when goals conflict.
_Avoid_: student, user (when this person is meant)

**Shorts Quitter**:
The secondary user: someone trying to stop losing time to Shorts but who may still watch some.
_Avoid_: Shorts addict

### What D-Tox changes

**Distraction**:
A part of YouTube's interface that D-Tox can hide, such as the home feed, the Shorts shelf or comments.
_Avoid_: element, feature, widget, section

**Setting**:
A person's choice to hide or keep one Distraction, or to switch a YouTube behaviour such as autoplay.
_Avoid_: toggle, option, flag

**Intention Page**:
What YouTube's home page becomes under D-Tox: a "What are you here for?" prompt with a search box, in place of the recommended feed.
_Avoid_: blank home, search-only mode, focus page

**Calm Default**:
The Settings a fresh install starts with: the Intention Page, Shorts hidden, autoplay off and watch-page recommendations hidden.
_Avoid_: preset, minimal mode, recommended settings

### Quality

**Baseline Run**:
An audit run of YouTube with no extension installed. Every measurement of D-Tox is compared with it.
_Avoid_: off run, control

**Release Gate**:
The check every release must pass: the audit with every Setting on, compared with a Baseline Run on lightness, page growth, timeouts and playback.
_Avoid_: perf test, QA pass
